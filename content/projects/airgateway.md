**Role:** brand, visual direction, UI and front-end · **Year:** 2026 · [Visit airgateway.com ↗](https://airgateway.com/)

### The brief

AirGateway sells to managed travel sellers: travel agencies, TMCs and consolidators who are moving their airline bookings from the GDS to NDC. The old website talked like a tech company. The new one needed to talk like a partner, explain a complex industry shift in one line, and make the products easy to find.

### One message, said loudly

The page opens with a single promise, **"Making NDC simple for managed travel sellers"**, followed by the proof in one sentence: real-time aggregated content for 40+ NDC and LCC airlines. A heavy, compact display face carries the headlines, a clean sans-serif carries the reading text, and AirGateway red is used only where it matters: the logo, the calls to action and the active states.

### A system of cards

Products, features, testimonials and news all share one visual language: crisp black outlines, hard offset shadows and small angled tags. It gives the site a confident, almost editorial feel and makes every block instantly recognisable as AirGateway. Feature blocks pair simple line icons with short, scannable copy so a busy agent understands "GDS capabilities with your direct NDC" in seconds.

### Trust, up front

Numbers people care about (IATA agencies, airline NDC credentials, travel consultants) sit right under the airline and partner logos, followed by tabbed testimonials from agencies, travel networks and partners.

![Key numbers and the tabbed testimonials](/images/airgateway-proof.jpg)

### A newsroom that keeps the site alive

Airline launches and partnerships are published as articles with a consistent cover template, so the marketing team can ship news fast without breaking the look.

![Latest news cards with templated airline covers](/images/airgateway-news.jpg)

### Answers and a footer that works hard

An accordion FAQ handles the questions sales hears every day, and the footer carries the full sitemap, live uptime badges for the platform and API, and compliance seals.

![FAQ accordion and footer](/images/airgateway-faq-footer.jpg)

### Mobile first, not mobile later

The same system scales down to a phone without losing its punch: the headline stays big, cards stack cleanly, and navigation folds into a simple menu.

![The airgateway.com homepage on a phone](/images/airgateway-mobile.jpg#phone)

### Designed and built

As with most of my work at AirGateway, I took the site from concept to code: research and moodboards (with AI in the loop), the visual system, the components and the front-end build, working closely with the founder and the dev team.
